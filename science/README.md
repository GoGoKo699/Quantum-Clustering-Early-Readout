# Scientific reading route

An online learner would like to classify the specimens already received and still make the best possible decision when more arrive. This project identifies the classical information that can be released while preserving the exact optimal eventual whole-partition score.

1. [The model](model.md) defines the Haar-pure two-template source, the uniform partition prior, the causal instrument and exact preservation criterion.
2. [The established global optimum](optimal_measurement.md) supplies an explicit dual. Its equality conditions cover every optimal continuation, rather than fixing a decoder in advance.
3. [Harmonic frames](harmonic_frames.md) identify the relevant spherical lines and prove their independence, including the balanced complement quotient.
4. [Temporal rigidity](temporal_rigidity.md) uses a branching annihilator lemma to show that a penultimate record is permutation-blind and any strictly earlier record carries no partition information.
5. [The complete free-readout family](free_readout.md) gives necessary and sufficient coefficients and one maximal classical experiment. The construction can be implemented causally with a local prefix instrument followed by conditional final readout.
6. [Prefix accuracy](prefix_accuracy.md) solves the resulting decision problem and its fixed-dimension asymptotic behavior.
7. [Finite-size stability](stability.md) separates the exact theorem from what has actually been established under a small allowed final loss.

The [interpretation](interpretation.md) develops the physical story. The [resource account](resources.md) states the supplied systems, storage, synthesis and ideal-control assumptions. [Related work](../references/RELATED_WORK.md) identifies the inherited clustering optimum, three-qubit tradeoff, thesis conjectures and general measurement-theoretic tools.

The complete classical-information characterization is modulo ensemble likelihood equivalence. It does not order the quantum output states of different instruments or establish a scalable approximately optimal learning prohibition.
